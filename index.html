A little about me

About

I like the moment when a pile of numbers (or a pile of Reddit threads) turns into a clear answer someone can act on. That’s why I mix data science with business context: machine learning, statistics, and a lot of writing it up so people can follow along.

Lately I’ve been digging into applied AI: LLM agents, evaluating LLM output, and forecasting from messy qualitative data. I also like building small web things like this one.
